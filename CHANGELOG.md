## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.0] - unreleased

Based on Arctic Fuse 3 v3.3.3 by jurialmunkey.

### Added
- Condensed fontset using Roboto Condensed (Light, Regular, Bold).
- Technical audio and file section in the music info dialog: path, codec, bitrate, sample rate, duration, BPM, file size, play count and rating.
- Clickable song and album folder paths in the music info dialog.
- Uncropped thumb fallback for items without fanart; the blur background falls back to artist fanart or thumb.
- Random genre option for widget sorting.
- Random Album Artists shortcut.
- Default view types per content type, including the YouTube add-on.

### Changed
- Font sizes reduced by about 20% across the skin, with tighter spacing.
- Micro-OSD: 64px circular controls, 10px progress bar and a custom seek-slider nib.
- Time, date and weather in the header use a consistent, smaller, normal-weight font.
- Skin music smart playlists moved to "Skin Music Playlists", so widgets built from them open the music library.
- Music widget heights adjusted for the smaller layout.

### Removed
- Spinning disc art in the music info dialog.
